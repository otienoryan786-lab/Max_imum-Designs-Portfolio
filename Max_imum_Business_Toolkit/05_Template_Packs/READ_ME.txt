SOCIAL TEMPLATE PACKS (1080 x 1080, square)
Two packs, 5 templates each: Food_and_Retail and Church_and_Events.
Open the .pptx in PowerPoint or Google Slides (File > Import) or upload to Canva (Create > Import file). All text is live and editable.
How to use: replace every [bracketed] item, swap the dashed photo boxes for your photo (right-click > Change picture),
change colours with Design/Format > Shape fill, then export each slide as PNG (File > Export). Delete the small "Template by" line if you do not want it.
Fonts: Arial, so it looks the same everywhere. In Canva, the font may switch to a similar one.
