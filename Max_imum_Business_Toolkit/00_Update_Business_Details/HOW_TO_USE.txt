UPDATE BUSINESS DETAILS: fill your details once, update every document and the website

WHAT IT DOES
Your Word documents (agreements, policies, WhatsApp guide, company profile and more) contain details written in CAPITALS in square brackets, like [BUSINESS ADDRESS].
This tool replaces them everywhere at once, so you do not have to edit 20 files. Your original documents are never changed. Filled copies are saved in the folder "Filled_Documents", so you can run the tool again whenever a detail changes.

THE DETAILS
[REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME]      name on your registration certificate
[BUSINESS REGISTRATION NO.]     your registration number
[KRA PIN]                       your KRA PIN (never shown on the website)
[BUSINESS ADDRESS]              postal or physical address you are happy to put on contracts and policies
[M-PESA TILL]                   your M-Pesa Till or Paybill (used in the WhatsApp guide and the demand letters)
[ODPC STATUS]                   one sentence on ODPC registration, written only after you have checked the ODPC thresholds (the Compliance Roadmap explains how)
[LAST UPDATED]                  date on the policies (if left empty, today's date is used)

STEPS (needs Python and python-docx)
1. Open a terminal in this folder and run:  pip install python-docx
2. Open business-details.json in any text editor. Type your details between the quotes. Save.
3. Run:  python update_business_details.py
4. Open the folder "Filled_Documents" for the updated Word files. Check them, then send or print.
5. Upload "Filled_Documents/Website_Upload/js/business-details.js" to the js folder in your website repository on GitHub (Add file > Upload files, commit). The privacy policy, policies page and footer on the website update by themselves.
To include the brand package documents (such as the editable company profile), add its folder when you run the tool:
   python update_business_details.py "path\to\Max_imum_Designs_Brand_Package"

NO PYTHON? DO IT BY HAND
In Word press Ctrl+H (Find and Replace), type the token (for example [BUSINESS ADDRESS]) in "Find what" and your detail in "Replace with", then Replace All. Repeat for each token and each document.
For the website, edit js/business-details.js directly on GitHub (click the file, then the pencil icon) and commit.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO
It does not change PDFs, images, PSD files, the pitch deck or the printed stationery. Re-export those from the source documents when needed. Invoices, quotations and receipts have blank lines for your M-Pesa and KRA PIN: write them in by hand or in a PDF editor.
This tool fills in details only. Have a Kenyan advocate review the legal documents.
