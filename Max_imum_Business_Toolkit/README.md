# Max_imum Designs Business Toolkit

00_Update_Business_Details: fill your business details once (registration number, address, KRA PIN, M-Pesa Till and more) and update every Word document and the website policy pages at once. Start with HOW_TO_USE.txt.

01_Legal_Documents (12 Word templates): client agreement, small-job terms, retainer, NDA, handover and copyright assignment certificate, portfolio consent, AI policy, subcontractor agreement, privacy policy, website terms, payment reminder and demand letters, legal and compliance roadmap.
02_Operations: creative brief, studio workflow SOP, WhatsApp Business guide, case studies, and the Business Tracker workbook (projects, hours, payments, data register, dashboard).
03_Website: portfolio website ready for GitHub Pages.
04_Motion: animated logo (MP4 in 3 shapes, GIF, stills).
05_Template_Packs: two editable social packs (PowerPoint) with PNG previews.
07_Company_Profile_Editable: the editable Word company profile (included so the tool fills it too).
06_Marketing_and_Growth: content calendar and lead tracker, marketing playbook, Brand Health Check lead magnet, learning roadmap.

## First 30 days
Week 1: register the business name, put your details in 00_Update_Business_Details/business-details.json and run the tool, set up WhatsApp Business. The website is live at https://otienoryan786-lab.github.io/Max_imum-Designs/ (re-upload index.html, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, privacy.html and terms.html from this folder to update the address tags).
Week 2: post the launch content; offer 3 launch clients a discount for testimonials; send the Brand Health Check to 10 businesses.
Week 3: use the Agreement, Brief, Tracker and Handover Certificate on every job from now on.
Week 4: build 2 real case studies; apply for the trademark search; have an advocate review the legal pack.

Launch campaign kit (posts, carousels, stories, reel, flyers, posters, QR cards, email, playbook) is in the brand package, folder 11_Promotional_Materials (also sent as its own zip).

Interactive website (Max the animated welcome buddy, online Brand Health Check, logo preview and quote builder) is part of 03_Website. The mascot files are in the brand package, folder 12_Mascot_Max.

IMPORTANT: the legal documents are templates, not legal advice. Have a Kenyan advocate review them before first use.
