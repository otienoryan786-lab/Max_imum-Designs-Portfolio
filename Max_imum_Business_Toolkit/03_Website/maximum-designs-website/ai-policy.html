AI and Third-Party Materials Policy

Max_imum Designs believes clients deserve to know how their work is made. This policy explains how we use technology and other people's materials.

1. How we use AI-assisted tools

  • We use AI tools for ideas, mood boards, mock-ups, resizing, background clean-up and production help. A person (the designer) makes the creative decisions and checks every design.

  • We do not feed a client's confidential or unreleased material into public AI tools without permission.

  • We do not use AI to copy a named living artist's style or another brand's logo.

  • If a Final Deliverable contains AI-generated elements, we tell the client and list them on the Handover Certificate.

  • Clients who do not want AI used on their project can say so in Schedule A, and we will work without it.

2. What clients should know

  • Legal protection for purely AI-generated images is uncertain in Kenya and many other countries. We create the logo, wordmark and brand systems by hand so that the core brand assets are original and protectable.

  • AI tools' terms of use differ. We choose tools whose terms allow commercial use.

3. Fonts, images, icons and mock-ups

  • We use fonts with free commercial licences (such as Google Fonts under the Open Font Licence) or fonts the client has bought.

  • Stock photos and icons are used only under licences that allow the client's use. We tell the client which ones, and who must pay for extended licences.

  • We do not use images or music "found on the internet" without permission.

4. Client materials

  • Clients must have the right to use anything they send us. We may ask for proof of permission for logos, photos of people and music.

  • Photos of identifiable people need the person's consent, especially children. We will not use them without confirmation.

5. Changes

We update this policy as laws and tools change. Date of this version: October 2026.
