These terms apply to jobs under KES 10,000 and are attached to every quotation. Larger projects also have a signed agreement. Accepting a quotation means accepting these terms.

1. What you get. The design and file formats listed on your quotation. Anything extra is quoted separately.

2. Payment. Jobs under KES 3,000 are paid in full before work starts. Other jobs need a 50% deposit to start and the balance before final files are sent. We accept M-Pesa and bank transfer. Overdue invoices (7 days or more) can incur 2% per month and pause work.

3. Revisions. 2 rounds of changes are included (3 for logos). Extra rounds are KES 500 each. A round is one message with all your changes together.

5. Ownership. We own the design until you have paid in full. When you pay in full, the copyright in the final approved design is transferred to you in writing on request, and we sign a short certificate. We keep sketches, unused concepts and working files.

6. Portfolio. We may show the finished design in our portfolio and social media unless you tell us not to before work starts.

7. Your materials. You confirm you have the right to use any logo, text or photo you send us.

8. Cancelling. Before we start: the deposit is refunded less 10%. After we start: the deposit is kept. After we send concepts, 75% is due; after final approval, 100%. No refunds after final files are delivered, but we will fix our own errors free for 14 days.

9. Rush jobs. 24-hour delivery costs +30% (minimum KES 500) and depends on our availability.

10. Limits. We do not guarantee sales or followers. Our responsibility is limited to the amount you paid for the job. Colours may look different in print.

11. AI and third-party materials. We may use AI-assisted tools and licensed assets, and we will tell you when AI-generated elements are in your design.

12. Disagreements. We will first talk, then use mediation. Kenyan law applies. Claims up to KES 1,000,000 may go to the Small Claims Court.
