Questions

Good to know


How do I pay?

By M-Pesa or bank transfer. A 50% deposit starts the job (jobs under KES 3,000 are paid in full). The balance is due before final files are sent.

How many revisions do I get?

2 rounds for most designs and 3 for logos. Extra rounds are KES 500 each.

Who owns the design?

You do, once you have paid in full. We sign a short certificate that transfers the copyright in the final approved design to you. We keep sketches, unused concepts and working files.

Do you use AI?

We may use AI-assisted tools for ideas and production, but a designer makes every creative decision. We tell you when AI-generated elements are in your design, and you can ask us not to use AI at all.

How fast can you deliver?

Social posts in 24 to 48 hours, logos in 3 to 7 days, brand kits in 10 to 21 days. Rush delivery in 24 hours is available for +30%.

Can you print my designs?

We deliver print-ready files and can arrange printing at cost with a trusted printer on request.
