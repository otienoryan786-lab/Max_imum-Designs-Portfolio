MAX, THE MAX_IMUM DESIGN BUDDY (mascot)
Max is the tile character from our logo: a violet body, a dark screen face, a lime underscore smile, and a blinking cursor antenna.
PNG/        5 poses on transparent backgrounds (Happy, Waving, Wow, Thinking, Talking), 1280 px wide
SVG/        5 poses as vector files (the M is converted to outlines, so no font is needed)
Stickers_WhatsApp/  6 stickers, 512 x 512 (WebP files are under 100 KB for WhatsApp sticker apps; use a sticker-maker app to import them)
Animation/  Max_Waving.gif (on Ink)
The live, animated Max is on the website (js/max.js): eyes follow the cursor, he blinks, waves, talks and reacts.
RULES: keep the colours (Ink, Violet, Lime), do not stretch or tilt, do not add shadows or outlines, give him clear space equal to his antenna height, and keep his words short, friendly and honest.
Use Max for: welcome messages, tips, stickers, story reactions, error pages and social posts. Use the logo, not Max, for formal documents, contracts and invoices.
