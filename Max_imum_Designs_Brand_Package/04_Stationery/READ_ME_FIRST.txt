MAX_IMUM DESIGNS STATIONERY - HOW TO PRINT
==========================================
Every item is its own PDF, in two versions:
Print_Shop_with_3mm_Bleed -> give THESE to a print shop. Page = finished size + 3 mm all round. No scaling.
Office_Print_No_Bleed     -> for an office/home printer. Print at 100% / "Actual size" (not "Fit to page").
Envelopes: C4 (229x324 mm) takes A4 flat; C3 (324x458 mm) takes A3 flat; DL (110x220 mm) takes A4 folded in three.
Paper: letterhead/invoice/receipt 100-120 gsm; cards 350 gsm matt; thank-you card 300 gsm; sticker on white vinyl.
Fill-in items (invoice, quotation, creative brief, receipt, notepad, ID badge) are completed by hand or typed over in a PDF editor.
Add your M-Pesa Till/Paybill and KRA PIN before printing. Prices in the deck are proposed launch prices.
